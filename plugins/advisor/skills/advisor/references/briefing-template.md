# Advisor briefing

Send the advisor this briefing. Fill every section. Paste evidence verbatim; do not paraphrase it.

## Checkpoint

One of: major decision, stuck, pre-completion, on request. One line on why you are asking now.

## User's request

The original request, verbatim. Then every later constraint or correction from the user, verbatim and in order.

## What has happened so far

A short chronological account: what you investigated, decided, changed, tried, and ruled out, with the reason for each.

## Evidence

Relevant tool output, verbatim: errors, stack traces, test output, command output, diff hunks. Trim unrelated parts and mark the cuts with `[...]`. Redact tokens, keys, and `.env` values.

## Current state

The output of `git status --short` and `git diff --stat`, the files you touched, and any half-done work.

## Questions for the advisor

Numbered, specific questions. For a decision: the options, their tradeoffs, and which one you lean to and why. For a stuck case: expected versus observed behavior and what you already ruled out. For pre-completion: what you verified and what you did not.

## What I need back

The kind of answer you want: a verdict on a plan, a hypothesis check, or a final check before done.

## Previous consult

If you consulted the advisor earlier in this task and cannot resume it, paste its last verdict and the recommendations you applied or skipped. Otherwise write `none`.
